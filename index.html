          How the score worksGreat, Good, OK, Poor and Bad

          Every product starts at 100. Points come off for too much sugar, fat or salt and for additives of concern, and a few come back for protein and fibre.

          Great is 90 or more, Good is 75 or more, OK is 50 or more, Poor is 25 or more, and Bad is below 25.

          Where our data comes fromOpen Food Facts and labels we check

          Products come from Open Food Facts, a free open database (ODbL), and from labels our testers type in from the pack. A missing value says Not available. We never guess.
