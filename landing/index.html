The score

A score you can read in a second

Points come off for too much sugar, salt and fat, and for some additives. Protein, fibre and fruit add points. When a value is missing we say so, we never guess.

Read how the score works

Every product starts at 100. Points come off for too much sugar, fat or salt and for additives of concern, and a few come back for protein and fibre.

Great is 90 or more, Good is 75 or more, OK is 50 or more, Poor is 25 or more, and Bad is below 25.

  • Great90 to 100Nothing to watch out for, and something good
  • Good75 to 89A solid everyday pick
  • OK50 to 74Fine now and then
  • Poor25 to 49Better as a treat
  • BadUnder 25Lots to watch out for

Honest about our data

  • Never a guess

    If a value is not on the label or in the database, we show Not Available, not a zero.

  • Open data

    Product facts come from Open Food Facts, a free database anyone can improve.

  • Checked against the pack

    We compare what the app says with real labels in 7-Eleven and fix what differs.
